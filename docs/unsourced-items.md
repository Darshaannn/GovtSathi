# Unsourced Items and Verification Audit

This document tracks items in portal application lifecycles where no specific government documentation or official circular could be definitively referenced at development time. Under GovGuide's strict truthfulness rules, any item without a direct official government source must **never be stated as a verified fact** and must be classified as `unverified` with the trust label: *"Not verified. The portal may be different."*
